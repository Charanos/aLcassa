4.0 What is never shown Redaction before storage
Redaction happens before a log line is written, not when it is displayed, so there is no stored copy to leak later. This is the design answer to the privacy risk the Rev 3.1 amendment introduced.
Customer fields are replaced by their type and length, which is enough to debug a shape without reading a person.
Named gap
Soft delete is a column on customer records. Retention and erasure automation is out of Phase 1 and is a named gap, not a silent one.
