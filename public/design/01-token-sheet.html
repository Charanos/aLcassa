1.0 Where the colour comes from
Neutrals are warm. A support console is read for eight hours at a stretch, and a cool grey field under long-form reading produces the grey fatigue that operations tools are known for. The ramp is built on a single hue angle so that page, panel and field differ in lightness alone.
Accents are muted and earthen rather than saturated. Six hues carry every meaning in the system, and each is dropped to roughly 55 percent chroma of its pure form so that a row of six chips does not vibrate against the page.
Both themes are generated from the same six hue angles. The dark theme is not an inversion: ink lightens, tints darken toward the page, and every ratio is measured again rather than assumed to have survived the flip.

3.0 The six hues, four variants each {{ hueCount }}
One hue, one meaning, four variants: ink for type and glyphs, solid for a filled control, tint for a status field, rule for its edge. Two accent hues never appear on one surface. Copper is the only hue permitted to carry interaction, which is why it is absent from the status vocabulary except for support mode.

5.0 Marks, faces and glyphs Drop an image on any slot
The system carries three kinds of image: a tenant's brand mark, a person's face, and a product still for documentation. Each has one shape and one fallback, and the fallback is designed rather than improvised, because most tenants will never upload anything.
